## Error type and passage invariants #142

Jessica: There's been a lot of discussion about errors in the implementers group, we don't have agreement yet, but there's a lot of discussion around Dave's proposal

Dave: I can read what's in the issue. I guess the first thing is we're referring to issue 142 which combines what we feel are two separate issues: whether or not there's an error data type in ocapn, and whether or not we restrict the data types that are allowed as broken promise values. So I guess there's an issue on the table, we haven't made separate issues for these, but I seem to remember that we had general agreement that making separate issues are good. And as a counter-proposal to what Kris opened in the issue. Our counter-proposal at Spritely has 3 basic points: 1) No introduction of an error type to ocapn, 2) no restrictions on broken promise values and 3) strong recommendations on what to do with broken promises and errors. The two ways promises are broken we're seeing, one is explicitly broken promises by user code, and the other case is unexpected behavior where an exception is raised. In the former, we believe the promises should be passed along as-is, and in the exceptional case, the *default* behavior should be to pass along an opaque value, and finally when there is explicit intention for debugging, capabilities can be provided as such

Kris: And the things that need to be mulled on the endo side are: how to associate an arbitrary passable value with appropriate diagnostic information when diagnostic information is handled in band, and the other is how to dehydrate and rehydrate errors in band.
